Getting here

Parking

100 First Garage is the closest parking garage. Be sure to reserve a spot in advance through ParkWhiz or SpotHero as it fills quickly in the morning.

Unfortunately, we do not validate or sponsor parking at this time. The typical cost for parking ranges from $20 to $35 for a half day. Allow extra time to park and walk.

Public transportation

Taking public transit or rideshare (Uber/Lyft/Waymo) is recommended — if you want to avoid parking downtown, you could opt to park in a local neighborhood (e.g. Potrero Hill) and take rideshare from there.

The nearest BART station is Montgomery Station, a 10 minute walk to the office. A bus stop is located right next to the office building. The Ferry Building is a 13 minute walk to the office.

When you arrive

Please check in with security at the front desk lobby. Your name will be on the security list and you will be directed to an elevator which will take you to the 17th floor, where our office is! Once in the lobby, check in with reception.

Checking in

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID (license, passport, or other government-issued ID) to verify your identity at check-in.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

Return your badge at the front desk when leaving.
