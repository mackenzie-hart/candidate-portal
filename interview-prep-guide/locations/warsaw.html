Getting here

Varso Place underground parking

Address: Chmielna 71, Warszawa

Located on levels -1 and -2 in Varso 1 and level -1 in Varso 2.

Parking rates:

  • Monday–Friday, 7:00–18:00: 10 PLN/hour
  • Monday–Friday, 18:00–7:00: 7 PLN/hour

The parking ticket can be paid by card at the automatic payment machine (automatyczny kasjer) or by card/cash at reception in the main lobby.

To reach the ground floor, take the “F” elevators to floor 0, then turn right toward reception.

Street parking

Street parking is available around the building. Payment can be made at a parking meter or via SkyCash / your bank’s mobile parking app (mobiParking).

When you arrive

Getting to the lobby

Head to the main Varso entrance at the Chmielna/Jana Pawła II intersection. Please use your QR code to access the elevators on the right of the main entrance (left to the reception). The QR code will automatically direct you to your reception at the 22nd floor.

Check-in

Once you arrive at the 22nd floor, check in with reception and let them know who you are meeting with first. You will be asked to input their name into our check-in software (Envoy) at the tablets at the front desk so that they are notified of your arrival. You may be asked to sign the NDA again. Your coordinator will meet you in the lobby and help you get started with your interviews.

Checking in

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID (license, passport, or other government-issued ID) to verify your identity at check-in.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival and departure

Please keep the printed QR code you received from the Varso Tower ground floor reception with you, as you may need it to exit through the gates on the ground floor.

During the interview

Laptop

Please bring a laptop if you are completing a technical assessment or presentation. If you need to borrow one, please let your Recruiter know.

Wi-fi

After signing in at the front desk, you will receive an email from Envoy that contains your personalized Wi-fi password. Choose the “Box Visitor” Wi-fi network, then sign in with the email you used to check in and your personalized password.

Lunch

You'll have a lunch break with the Hiring Manager or future team members. You will receive a 30 PLN lunch card for Salad Story or Meet & Eat, both located on the main floor of the office building.

Please note that we do not cover any lunch expenses past 30 PLN.
