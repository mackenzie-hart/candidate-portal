Zoom (virtual)

Where to join

At the time of your interview, please join the Zoom meeting via the link located in your Google Calendar invitation. If you do not see a link, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Setting up for your interview

Your environment

Find a quiet, private space with good lighting. Confirm the Zoom link is working — we recommend downloading the desktop application. Test your camera, microphone, speakers and internet connection ahead of time. Close extra tabs or applications to avoid notifications or lag.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

Joining your interview

You will be automatically placed in a waiting room and will need to be admitted by the first interviewer. If you leave the session at any time, you will need to be admitted again.

AI Notetakers

The use of Zoom AI features or any personal recording, transcription, or automated note-taking tools during live interviews is not permitted at this time.

Your interviewer may use BrightHire to record and transcribe interviews for quality purposes and to ensure an engaging interview experience. Your consent to such recording is entirely optional and will not affect your candidacy. If you prefer to not have your interview recorded, you may opt-out of the recording by clicking here or by asking the meeting host to not record at the start of your interview.

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
